Poligon App

From client registration to session management.

A desktop application designed to give staff a faster, more organized and easier-to-follow workflow throughout daily operations, from client registration to digital evidence keeping and the use of membership cards.

Lane overview

See lane status and open session details from the same operational screen.

Firearms and session details

Keep relevant session information organized and connected to the client.

04

Signature and evidence book

After the client finishes shooting, the signature is captured on a digital device and stored in the evidence book digitally for cleaner documentation.

  • The signature is linked to the related shooting session and client
  • The evidence record is stored more clearly and in a more organized way
  • A device such as Signotec Sigma can be used to capture the signature

New feature

NFC identification without manually typing the personal number.

An Android phone can be used as an NFC reader. When the document is supported, Poligon App receives identity data and the photo from the ID card, speeding up registration and reducing manual data entry.

✓

ID card data

✓
Photo from the document

The application can also receive the photo from a supported identity document.
